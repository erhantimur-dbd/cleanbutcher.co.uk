# The Drove — brand sheet

For founders, a designer, and butcher conversations. The full argument is in `brand-and-marketplace-plan.md`.

## Name

**The Drove**

A drove is the old British route livestock walked from grazing country to market. We rebuilt that path: named farms, through butchers we approve, to the door.

Say it “the drove”. URL `thedrove.co.uk`. Company The Drove Ltd (subject to search).

Not Clean Butcher. Not a directory. Not Ethical Butcher with a different adjective.

**Backup if UKIPO blocks it:** Well Grazed.

## Promise (one sentence)

Clean British meat, from butchers we have actually approved.

## Masterline

The Drove. From pasture, through butchers we trust, to your door.

## Category

Approved clean meat, delivered. UK only. Online only.

## Who it is for

Households who already pay more for organic or farm-shop meat and still do not trust a steak. Butchers who already cut to that bar and cannot be a national website.

## Personality

Calm, specific, adult. Pastoral and precise. Firm about farming, warm to butchers, never smug.

Not a lecture. Not a steak club. Not a wellness brand.

## Voice

Use: pasture, named, approved, British, outdoor, traceable, slow-grown, doorstep.

Do not use: grass-fed (unless 100% and evidenced), clean eating, artisan, gourmet, finest, passion, journey, disrupt, carbon negative, ethical-as-badge.

Show the rule. Name the farm. Name the hanging days.

## Look (brief for a designer)

Cartographic pastoral. Routes and land, not butcher stripes.

- Paper, deep pasture green, iron, oxblood accent
- British serif wordmark + a path mark (not an animal)
- Photography in weather: real farms, real tickets, real parcels
- Outer pack is The Drove. Inner card is the butcher and the farm

Forbidden: black-and-gold luxury, hessian cliché, sage wellness, stock rosemary.

## Architecture

| Layer | Role |
| --- | --- |
| The Drove | Consumer brand, seller of record |
| The Drove Standard | The bar butchers fail or pass |
| Drove Butchers | Named makers, never generic sellers |
| The Drove Box | The habit |
| Drove Notes | The proof, in writing |

On a product: *The Drove ribeye · cut by [Butcher] · from [Farm], [county]*.

## Customer lines

- Meat as it should be.
- Named farms. Approved butchers. UK only.
- If we cannot tell you how it was raised, we will not sell it.

## Butcher lines

- Join the Drove.
- You cut. You ship. We bring the households.
- A national shop window. You keep the craft.

## What we never look like on the site

A marketplace grid of random shops. The customer shops one brand. The butcher is credited like a maker.
