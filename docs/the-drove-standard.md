# The Drove Standard — v1 draft

Public bar for any butcher who wants to join. This is a planning draft, not a legal policy. Freeze it in Phase 0 after butcher and food-law review.

If a product cannot meet this, The Drove will not sell it. Popularity does not override it.

## All species

- UK-reared and UK-cut. No imported meat. No “packed in the UK” loophole.
- Named farm, or a named producer group with a public list.
- Named butcher and a live cutting site.
- No routine antibiotics. Treatment of a sick animal is allowed, with withdrawal as in organic practice.
- No cages, no farrowing crates, no zero-graze beef.
- Traceability: farm → abattoir → cutting plant → parcel.
- Animals stunned before slaughter.
- Cutting site: registered food business, FHRS 4 or 5, relevant approvals, insurance, recall process.
- The butcher ships and guarantees temperature-controlled delivery to The Drove SLA.

## Beef

100% pasture-fed for life (Pasture for Life or equivalent documented diet). Organic preferred. Dry-aged on the bone for a published minimum (21–28 days recommended).

**Say:** 100% pasture-fed. Organic where certified.  
**Do not say:** “grass-fed” alone. UK law allows that at 51% grass.

## Lamb, hogget, mutton

Same pasture rule. Seasonal. Name the season.

## Pork

Outdoor-bred and outdoor-reared. Organic **or** a published high-welfare pasture system. No crates. No routine antibiotics. Soy in feed disclosed; prefer soy-free or organic soy.

**Do not say:** grass-fed pork.

## Chicken, turkey, goose

Organic **or** slow-grown with genuine pasture access. No routine antibiotics. Publish growth days.

**Do not say:** grass-fed chicken.

## Game

UK wild, legally taken, named estate or game dealer.

## Seals on the page, in this order

1. Soil Association (or other organic certifier) where true  
2. Pasture for Life where true  
3. The Drove Standard mark — clearly *our* rule, not a fake cert  
4. Farm, county, butcher  
5. Cutting-site hygiene rating  

Ruminant meat that is neither organic nor 100% pasture-fed does not go in the catalogue.

## Probation

Approval is not a toggle. First 20 live orders must clear without a temperature or claims failure. Quarterly review. Fail the standard, leave the Drove.
