# The Drove

UK-only, online-only marketplace brand for clean meat: organic, 100% pasture-fed beef and lamb, no routine antibiotics. Approved butchers join, sell at wholesale, ship and guarantee delivery. The Drove sells to households at retail and keeps the spread.

This repository holds the **plan only**. Nothing has been built.

**Read this:** [docs/brand-and-marketplace-plan.md](docs/brand-and-marketplace-plan.md)

## Why this brand

Donald Russell is the most popular UK online butcher by scale (trust, frozen consistency, 50 years, Royal Warrant). Pipers & Co and Field & Flower lead the values niche. Nobody owns a **national consumer brand** with a hard clean-meat bar, fulfilled by many approved butchers.

The working name `cleanbutcher.co.uk` describes the job. It is not a brand. **The Drove** is: the old British path from pasture to market, rebuilt for the doorstep.

## Do not start with

- An open “butchers sign up” marketplace
- Soft “grass-fed” claims (UK law allows 51% grass)
- Taking last-mile delivery in-house (see Farmdrop)
- A website before the standard, six butchers, and the margin are real
